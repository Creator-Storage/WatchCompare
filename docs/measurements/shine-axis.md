# Badge shine axis — exact-frame measurement

The second badge shine was normalized into the canonical badge coordinate space and measured on consecutive strong-shine source frames. Weighted PCA of the bright residual band gives a stable diagonal axis around **120 degrees**.

Strong-frame fit summary:

- mean axis: **119.938°**
- median axis: **119.780°**
- standard deviation: **0.708°**
- representative per-frame fits cluster roughly from **119.2° to 121.3°**

This strongly supports a designed **120° band axis**, but the compositor must still prove the exact raster result. Therefore:

- the measured axis is considered locked evidence;
- 120° is the current geometric model;
- shine width, falloff and intensity remain provisional until localized image diffs pass against the decoded source frames.

The source badge rasters remain local; only these derived measurements are committed.
